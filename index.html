My Dearest Damy,

My Anje Baby,

I don't think I tell you enough how much you mean to me. Every day, I find myself grateful that you're in my life. You bring so much happiness, comfort, and love into my world that sometimes it's difficult to put into words.

No matter how good or bad my day is, talking to you always makes everything better. Your smile, your laugh, your little habits, and even the things you don't realize you do make me fall for you more and more. You have a way of making ordinary moments feel special just by being there.

Thank you for being patient with me, for supporting me, and for loving me even when I'm not at my best. Knowing that I have you gives me strength and motivation to keep moving forward.

Anje Baby, I want you to know that you are deeply loved, appreciated, and cherished. You are not just my girlfriend—you are my best friend, my safe place, and one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

I look forward to all the memories we'll create together, all the laughs we'll share, and all the challenges we'll overcome side by side. No matter what happens, I will always treasure what we have.

I love you more than words can truly express, Damy. Thank you for being you.

Forever yours,

Arief, your baby boo

Out of everyone in this world, I'd choose you.
